Hover an earlier message, or use the row under the latest answer, and click the translate icon.
The card under the message shows the detected language and the translation; the close button hides it.

The icon appears on finalized messages that contain text.
A streaming reply gets it when the reply settles.

Translation runs in the browser against `translate.googleapis.com`; clicking sends that message's text to Google.
Nothing is sent before the click.

[Setup and limits](https://github.com/miorin-chan/dsh-message-translate#readme)
